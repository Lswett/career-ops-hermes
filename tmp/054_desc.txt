ABOUT US:
Auto Hauler Exchange (AHX) is an innovative startup revolutionizing the auto transport and logistics industry. Our platform connects vehicle shippers and carriers to streamline vehicle transportation with real-time tracking, transparent pricing, and an easy-to-use interface. As we continue to grow, we are looking to add another experienced 
Full-Stack Engineer
 to our software development team in Rochester, MI.
JOB DESCRIPTION:
As a key member of the software development team, our team of Full Stack Engineers build our cutting-edge web applications from start to finish, working on the front-end, back-end, and infrastructure. Our Full Stack Engineers are responsible for the entire project, from conception to deployment.
RESPONSIBILITIES (include, but are not limited to):
Build upon our proprietary platform and integrate into a headless commerce system.
Work with development teams and product managers to ideate software solutions.
Work with stakeholders during implementation for testing, debugging, support, and maintenance.
Write effective and intuitive APIs for client, customer, and partner developers.
Develop and manage well-functioning databases and applications.
Design client and server architecture for cloud deployment.
Build front-ends with intuitive appealing visual design.
Build mobile responsive user experiences.
Test software to ensure responsiveness and efficiency.
Troubleshoot, debug, and upgrade software.
Create security and data protection settings.
Continuously focus on leveraging AI in order to boost productivity, improve code quality, enhance security, and create more adaptive and innovative applications.
ABOUT YOU:
A minimum of 5 years of proven experience as a Full Stack Developer.
A bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a relevant field.
Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
Great attention to detail and excellent organization skills.
Knowledge of front-end languages and libraries (e.g. HTML/ CSS, JavaScript, Next.js, React, Tailwind, Radix).
Knowledge of back-end languages and libraries (e.g. Python, Node.js, Rest, GraphQL).
Experience with Cloud Platforms (e.g. AWS, GCP, or Azure).
Experience leveraging AI tools such as GitHub Copilot, Codeium, and Tabnine.
Familiarity with databases (e.g. MySQL, Postgres).
Experience developing mobile apps (e.g. React Native, iOS - Swift, Android).
Experience with UX/UI product or design tools (e.g. Figma > CSS / HTML or Mobile).
Hands-on experience standing up BigQuery as an analytics platform: dataset and table design, partitioning and clustering, streaming and batch ingestion, and query cost controls.
Experience with the GCP data stack: Datastream or comparable replication, Pub/Sub subscriptions into BigQuery, Dataform or dbt for transformation, and a catalogue and lineage tool such as Dataplex.
Experience implementing warehouse governance: row-level or column-level security, IAM scoping, retention and deletion paths, and quotas set before a query surface opens up.
